Sources, method and gaps

  • Annual prices: RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian Economy, "Average Price of Gold and Silver in Domestic and Foreign Markets" — 2009-10 ed. Table 41 (1970-71 to 1974-75), 16 Sep 2016 ed. Table 42 (1975-76 to 1984-85), 2025-26 ed. Table 35 (1985-86 to 2025-26). Mumbai, April–March averages; gold Rs/10 g, silver Rs/kg. RBI: data up to 1999-2000 and from 2000-01 "may not be strictly comparable" (source change).
  • 1946–1969: no annual gold average could be read on a government site. Dated points come from RBI's Report on Currency and Finance 1958-59 (read on a non-government mirror of RBI's site) and from the Finance Minister in the Lok Sabha, 21 Dec 1964. These are not annual averages and are never interpolated.
  • 2026: monthly only (Handbook Table 164 to Apr 2026; Bulletin Table 21 May–Aug 2026). No annual 2026-27 figure is shown.
  • Worked-out values: YoY %, premium (Mumbai ÷ RBI world price in rupees − 1), ratio (gold Rs/10 g × 100 ÷ silver Rs/kg) and the world/rupee/premium split are computed in this file from RBI columns. Duty equivalents are time-weighted from the confirmed duty history; June 2013 and July 2019 changes assumed on the 1st.
  • Exchange rate: RBI official rates to 1991-92; RBI-published market-based rates from 1992-93; Economic Survey 2025-26 for 2018-19 to 2024-25; RBI Annual Report 2025-26 for 2025-26.
  • Inflation: WPI annual rates from RBI tables; 2025-26 WPI (0.7%) is provisional.
  • Verification: an independent check re-compared all 448 RBI annual values, every exchange-rate, monthly and SGB figure against the research notes and recomputed every derived value; no numeric mismatch was found.
  • Not charted (incomplete government series): yearly monsoon rainfall, repo-rate history, gold imports before 1997-98. See the written report.
