    Walk-forward backtest (§3.6)

    Every forecast at date t uses only data up to t. M0 = no change (identical to the app's default zero-trend, no-shock forecast, so not reported separately). M1 = expanding-window average log-change × T. M2 = M1 + duty pass-through for duty changes already in force at t (λ defaults; equilibrium slope re-fitted on data up to t). M3 = range coverage of M1 with a rolling 10-year covariance ending at t. Every figure sits beside the no-change benchmark and its n.
